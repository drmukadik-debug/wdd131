Site Name

www.ehna-drc org

This name reflects the organization's commitment to emergency healthcare, nutrition, and community development in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Site Purpose

The website provides information about a nonprofit organization based in Mbujimayi, Democratic Republic of the Congo. It introduces the organization, explains its mission, vision, values, and health and nutrition programs, shares news and community outreach activities, encourages volunteer and partner participation, and offers an online contact form for visitors to connect with the organization.

Scenarios

  • How can I volunteer with EHNA?
  • Where can I receive emergency medical care from EHNA?
  • What emergency medical services does EHNA provide?
  • How can I partner with or support EHNA?
